# Spike — Free Offline Installer

![Category](https://img.shields.io/badge/Email_Client-blue?style=flat-square)

## About

Spike transforms email into a messaging-style chat experience. Emails between Spike users appear as instant messages; traditional emails appear as collapsible threads. With collaborative documents, video calls, and team channels built on email infrastructure, Spike reimagines email as a modern team communication tool while remaining compatible with standard email clients.

## Common Problems & Limitations

- **Account Required** — Full experience requires recipient to also use Spike
- **Email Paradigm Change** — Chat-style email experience requires adjustment from traditional email
- **Commercial Subscription** — Team features require paid plan
- **Enterprise Adoption** — Limited adoption means most correspondents still use traditional email clients
